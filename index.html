How signals work

EMA 9 vs EMA 21, MACD vs its signal line, and RSI each contribute one directional vote. RSI above 70 or below 30 is treated as neutral rather than automatically bullish or bearish. Scores of +2/+3 produce UP; -2/-3 produce DOWN; other scores produce NEUTRAL.

Historical test: each prior candle's indicator signal is compared with the following closed candle's direction. This is a limited next-candle direction test, not a broker-specific payout or a verified trading edge.
