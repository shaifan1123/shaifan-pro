How the score works

Three indicator votes are used: EMA 9 vs EMA 21, MACD vs signal, and RSI position. A score of +2 or +3 gives UP; -2 or -3 gives DOWN; all other combinations give NEUTRAL. This is a simple rule-based indicator score, not a forecast probability.

RSI above 70 or below 30 is treated as an extreme reading and does not automatically count as a directional vote. The latest unfinished candle is excluded from the indicator calculation.
